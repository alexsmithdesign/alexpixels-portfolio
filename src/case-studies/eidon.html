Eidon

APP DESIGN, WEB DESIGN, BRANDING

The platform started as a cross-platform progressive web app for collecting multimodal AI training data at scale. It grew to over 10,000 users who collectively gathered terabytes of image, audio, and video data through task-based 'quests.' Designed systems for real-time ingestion, quality validation, and pipeline processing.

THE APP WAS THE MAIN PROJECT

When Eidon first approached me their app had a lot of potential, but there was no visual language at all. It looked very much like a programmer made it. No grid, no alignment, no interesting fonts, no accessibility, and very bad UX. The turn around for the project was pretty fast, so I immediately got to work doing wireframes and also doing visual moodboards. I didn't have time to do a full brand guideline for Eidon, but I shared explorations and examples using their colors and got approval. Next I moved onto turning the wireframes into fully finalized visuals. Even though I couldn't do a brand guideline, I did my best to maintain the component library of the app for visual consistency. After the launch of the updated app, usage of the app quadrupled. Less users were dropping off, and there were fewer negative reviews. I attribute this to the strong user experience and is enhanced by strong consistent visuals. Through things like visual hierarchy, color theory, layout, scale, accessibility, and typography the user is able to more easily navigate without developing a negative impression of both the product and brand.

BRAND GUIDELINES

After completing my work for Eidon they began to pivot towards AI and Robotics combined, so I decided to speculate what it would look like if I continued creating designs for their brand by creating hypothetical guidelines and website.

LOGO MODIFICATION

The font originally paired with the Logo Mark was wrong for the cutting edge AI company, so I decided to make a custom set of letters.

MOBILE WEB DESIGN

The mobile website was treated as its own design challenge. It's not a scaled-down version of the desktop experience, but a purpose-built interface optimized for touch, speed, and focus. Navigation was simplified, content was restructured for vertical reading, and interactive elements were sized and spaced for confident one-handed use.
